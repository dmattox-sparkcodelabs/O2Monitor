Chart controls: scroll wheel to zoom, click-and-drag to pan, preset buttons for fixed windows, scrubber to slide a fixed window through the night. ODI3 / ODI4: oxygen desaturation index — events per hour where SpO2 drops 3% / 4% from a 100-second rolling baseline, sustained ≥10 seconds. Time below 88% and 90% are the standard clinical thresholds. Night labeling: a "night" runs noon-to-noon and is named by the wake morning.
